Positioning note: COSMO is not the market maker, not a trading strategy,
not a DEX and not a custodian. It is the mandate, control, execution and
proof layer between a capital owner and the operator who works with that
capital. Built on Supra; complementary to SupraOS and SupraFX.

PUBLIC SHORT VERSION
COSMO lets market makers and liquidity agents deploy third-party capital
without requiring blind trust. A capital owner issues one bounded mandate
covering venues, assets, amounts, slippage, gas, action count and term. The
mandate is sealed by hash, the policy is pinned before execution, a human
arms the irreversible step, exactly one transaction is submitted, settlement
is checked against what was signed, and a signed receipt closes the case. A
public standalone verifier re-checks the whole case from the files alone.
The mechanism has been proven with real capital on Ethereum mainnet,
including a complete mandate lifecycle through to termination, and across
two economic domains on Supra.
