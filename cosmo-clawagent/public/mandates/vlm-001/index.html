Honesty section — what this page cannot show you (yet), and why

  • The JIT funding transaction hashes were not archived; the wei-exact match is proven by balance reads on two RPCs, not by tx receipts.
  • The custody window's duration in seconds is unknown — only the empty states on both sides are proven.
  • "EUR 40" is the sum of the pinned budget legs — a cap, not a computed market value of the position.
  • Seed destruction cannot be proven, only attested: disk remanence and RAM copies are unprovable. The record says so verbatim and is graded attested_falsifiable — the weakest class it will ever carry. The falsification rule (any nonce above 4) is public and watched.
  • That no transaction was pre-signed before the seed was shredded is a process statement (the engine signs only on the ceremony path; the journals show no other sign events) — named, not claimed. Session keys or account abstraction would make this provable; that is a later mandate design, not this one.
  • The ETH gas remainder in the machine wallet (0.004914 ETH) was COSMO-financed; it is reported, not swept, and lies outside the "capital exposure" claim, which is scoped to sponsor capital in mandate assets.
  • The dedicated sweep-proof verb could not prove the close proceeds by shape (multicall via the position manager); receipt is shown from the settlement evidence and the transfer logs on two RPCs instead.
  • Public RPC quality shaped the day: one free endpoint answered 40 % of requests with a routing error, another refuses historical state, a third rate-limits bursts. The reads went through read-only retry proxies (one port = one upstream identity, broadcast methods refused) so that the two sources named in the record are truthfully two.
  • The verifier 1.3.1 published on 2026-08-22 did not carry the live pins; the 1.3.2 republish (2026-08-28) fixed that additively. The embedded-profile REJECT runs are archived alongside the ACCEPTs, deliberately.
