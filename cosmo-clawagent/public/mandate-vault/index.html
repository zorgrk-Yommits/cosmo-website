An agent manages this vault. The contract decides what it may do.

The owner deposits capital and sets a mandate. The agent provides liquidity with it, and a backer stands behind the agent with a bond that pays the owner first if the agent loses money. Every limit of the mandate is checked by the vault contract on each trade, and this page recomputes all of it from the public chain while you read.

Who can do what

Capital owner

Deposits and withdraws, to the owner address only. Sets, replaces and revokes the mandate. Pauses the vault.

Agent

Posts and cancels the quote the vault trades at. Rebalances through one venue. Records decisions on-chain.

Cannot withdraw, name a recipient, change the mandate, or lift a pause.

Backer

Posts the bond and thereby accepts exactly one mandate. Receives a share of a gain and, at the settlement, what is left of the bond.

Cannot take the bond back while the mandate runs. The agent key cannot move it either.

Anyone

Trades against the standing quote at the oracle price plus the edge. Freezes the agent once the vault value is below the floor. Triggers the settlement once the mandate has ended. Verifies everything.

Every check, recomputed here

The checks below ran in your browser against the public chain API. They use the mandate file and no data from the operator of the vault. The same file runs on the command line:

node verify/verify.mjs --mandate mandates/mandate-003.json --deployment mandates/deployment-003.json

What this proves: the published code is the code that runs, the on-chain mandate is the one in the file, and every quote, fill, swap, deposit, withdrawal, bond and settlement reproduces from its own event and respects every limit. What it does not prove: that the oracle price equals a market price, or that the strategy earns money.

Limits of this demonstration

  • The bond asset cvCOSMO is a test token that stands in for wCOSMO. It is not wCOSMO. On a real market a bond is worth what the market of the bonded token is worth.
  • The owner may revoke while the result is negative and is then paid from the bond at the price of that moment. The backer accepts this when posting the bond.
  • Testnet. The two assets are test tokens without value. The price is the real Supra oracle feed for SUPRA/USDT.
  • Most takers are two test bots run by the vault operator. Figures show that the mechanism works, not what a vault would earn.
  • The vault trusts the oracle price. A taker who sees a price move before the oracle does can trade against the old price; the mandate bounds this through the accepted oracle age, the trade size and the daily volume.
  • The package can still be upgraded by its publisher key. The check C9 fails as soon as that happens. A mainnet version needs the upgrade right removed or placed under a multisig.
  • One owner, one pair, one strategy. No audit.
