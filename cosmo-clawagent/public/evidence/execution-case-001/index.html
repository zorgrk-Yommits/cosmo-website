What verification checks (K/S/T/P/M/V/J/E/D/O)

  • K — attestor key pin: the signing key is pinned in the trust store.
  • S — receipt signature: ed25519 over the canonical envelope + statement.
  • T — time bounds: attestation time within sane limits.
  • P — policy pin: the policy hash matches the pinned, active policy.
  • M — mandate integrity: recomputed hash, signature, case and policy binding.
  • V — evidence integrity: manifest vs. directory, exact set, per-file sha3.
  • J — journal chain: every entry hash-chained, opened first, closed last.
  • E — execution envelope: BCS decode, local signature verify, mandate linkage.
  • D — derivation equality: the receipt statement re-derives byte-identically from the evidence.
  • O — outcome consistency: outcome matches the evidence the case actually produced.
