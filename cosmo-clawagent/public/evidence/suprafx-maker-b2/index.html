Capital was bounded by the venue delegate's caps, not by trust in the runner.

What was checked, and by what

  • G1–G10 (preflight, read-only) — code pinned and tests green on the bound commit; policy, gates and SDK modules hash-pinned; chain id and lock-release state; one dry decision pass; rate/lock/margin sanity; funds; exactly the expected delegate; policy read; empty ledger, no arm; iUSDC backing ratio from an own L1 read.
  • P-* (policy check, read-only) — the venue delegate is active, bound to the master, maker-only, pair restriction carried by the asset caps, SUPRA cap 15,000 with room, iUSDC cap 3, every other asset 0, expiry known.
  • V-1M … V-8M (per RFQ) — the maker gates on each RFQ: side and pair, rfq state and remaining lifetime, rate vs. floor, lock vs. cap, funds, delegate set, venue attestation freshness; a FAIL is named, not skipped (see the V-3M row).
  • FP1–FP4 (per fill) — RFQ matched with our quote accepted; SUPRA delta = lock minus rebate and iUSDC delta = size, exact in quants; no other asset moved; no lock and no open order left.
  • MB1–MB12 (offline re-verify of a bundle, 46 checks) — hashes cover every file; pins match the repository; intent signature and trigger row; rate is the formula rate on the stored reads; preflights and zero-capital recomputed; provenance of every read; replay of every decision bound to the intent; manifest equals the last decision; arm valid at decision time; executed event equals the FINAL AUTHORIZED decision by the armed delegate; ledger consistent; fill proof recomputed from the stored before/after reads.

Honest limits

· This is a self-attestation by the COSMO runner. The offline re-verification checks internal consistency against the venue reads the runner stored, not world truth, and the verifier program (commit 975241d) is not yet published; the checks it runs are listed above.

· All four RFQs came from one taker address. This proves a repeatable maker chain, not market depth and not a second counterparty.

· Economic size is pilot size: 2 iUSDC per fill at fair minus 50 bps, roughly 0.01 USD margin per fill.

· Two named deviations from the written policy were accepted in the GO text, because the venue's delegate form has no pair field and no expiry field: the pair restriction is carried by the asset caps (only SUPRA and iUSDC are capped, every other asset 0), and the delegate's venue expiry (~84 days) is longer than the window. Since the operator's decision after window 2, the delegate stays active between windows; the capital bound is the venue cap (15,000 SUPRA / 3 iUSDC, maker only, no withdrawal), plus the runner's own policy.

· The runner decides one case per 60-second tick. Fill 5 did not happen because no new iUSDC/SUPRA RFQ appeared between 12:06 and the 13:00 cutoff, not because of a refusal or an error.

· Fingerprints here are SHA-256 (the bundles' own convention); older evidence folders on this site use SHA3-256.

· Windows 1 and 2 (16.10., one fill each, −13,542.71 and −13,560.53 SUPRA for +2 iUSDC each) are recorded in the repository and shown in the showcase video; their bundles are not published on this page.
