WHAT THIS PROVES (scope, honest)
  One capital mandate with frozen limits (caps, action list, fee ceiling, expiry) was executed by a machine
  wallet: position opened and minted directly to the sponsor, approvals reset to zero, position closed with
  proceeds paid directly to the sponsor. After the expiry a read-only termination proof at a pinned terminal
  block on two independent RPCs derived five predicates (exposure zero, approvals zero, authority ended,
  ownership with the sponsor, every nonce 0..3 mapped to an archived receipt). The seed was then shredded
  and the ONE unprovable claim -- "no copy remains" -- is recorded as an attestation with a public
  falsification rule (any nonce above 4 on the execution wallet) and a standing 6-hour watch.
  It is an offline verification of internal consistency against the published trust profile, not a
  third-party attestation. Seed destruction and the absence of pre-signed transactions are attested /
  stated, not proven -- the records say so verbatim (unverifiable_assumptions).
