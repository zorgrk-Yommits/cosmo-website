What verification checks (K/S/T/P/M/V/J/E/D/O)

  • K — attestor key pin: the signing key is pinned in the trust store.
  • S — receipt signature: ed25519 over the canonical envelope + statement.
  • T — time bounds: attestation time within sane limits.
  • P — policy pin: the policy hash matches the pinned, active policy.
  • M — mandate integrity: recomputed hash, signature, case and policy binding.
  • V — evidence integrity: manifest vs. directory, exact set, per-file sha3.
  • J — journal chain: every entry hash-chained, opened first, closed last.
  • E — execution artifact: pure ed25519 order check (no SDK), sender/mandate/correlation binding.
  • D — derivation equality: the receipt statement re-derives byte-identically from the evidence.
  • O — outcome consistency: outcome matches the evidence the case actually produced.

Result for BOTH cases: ACCEPT, 10/10 criteria, exit code 0 — separate offline verifications of internal consistency, run with the private COSMO verifier.

Honest limits

· These receipts are self-attestations by the COSMO engine: one attestor, no independent attestor network.

· Verification proves internal consistency and record integrity — not world truth.

· Both ceremonies were armed by the same operator (operator:zorg). The separation of buyer and provider authority is cryptographic — distinct keys, one-shot mandates and domain policies — not organizational.

· The buyer wallet is agent-held but was created and funded by the founder for this drill; this is a staged drill with curated participants, not an external buyer.

· The signed artifacts are the deliver-order and approve-order (pure ed25519, offline-verifiable without any SDK), not raw chain tx bytes; the world anchors are the on-chain result_hash and the SETTLED escrow release.

· Supervised agent execution with explicit human authorization at each irreversible boundary — the human ARM step is part of the proven authority model, not a shortcut.

· Full 10/10 verification currently requires the private COSMO verifier.
